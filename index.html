Strategies
#1
Debuff First
Credit: Winter · "Most common, still very effective."
Poison Stun Hitters
▸
T1 · Poison / Curse
Lowest-level cards. Die fast, trigger debuffs on the whole enemy team.
T2 · Stun / Sleep
Exploit the debuffs. Enemy loses turns while taking bonus damage.
T3 · Main Hitters
Carry + Guard arrive to a debuffed, weakened opponent. Arena is won here.
T4 · Second Offense
Mops up survivors. Second strongest team.
Power order: Lowest → Mid → Highest → Higher-Mid
#2
Big Early
Credit: Winter · "More effective at higher power."
Debuff Big T2
▸
T1 · Small Debuffs
Poison and curse land early while the enemy is fresh.
T2 · Big Hitters
Main damage team hits while debuffs are at maximum freshness.
T3 · Mid Debuff Mix
All debuff types — stun, sleep, poison, curse — keep pressure on.
T4 · Second Biggest
Finishes survivors.
#3
Wall First
Credit: Winter · "Requires being more leveled."
Wall Debuff Hitters
▸
T1 · Endurance Wall
Tanky SSRs absorb damage. Opponent burns their resources on a wall.
T2 · Debuff Line
Full debuff team weakens survivors from T1.
T3 · Main Hitters
Strongest team arrives to a battered, debuffed opponent.
T4 · Finish
Mop up what's left.
#4
Bait & Punish
Sacrifice T1 to bait their strongest team, then punish.
Bait Punish
▸
T1 · Bait
1–2 weakest SSRs. Opponent burns Curse Assault and AoE cooldowns on almost nothing.
T2 · Stun / Sleep
Slows their T2 while they recover momentum.
T3 · The Punish
Your carries hit a fatigued opponent out of cooldowns. Maximum efficiency.
T4 · Cleanup
Opponent has no answers left.
#5
Poison Stack
Double wave of curse/poison. Curse Assault hits at peak multiplier.
×2 Curse 6×
▸
T1 · Curse Wave 1
6 curse/poison cards. Every death = another debuff stack on the enemy team.
T2 · Curse Wave 2
Double-stack. Enemy enters T3 at maximum debuff saturation.
T3 · Curse Assault Peak
Curse Assault deals up to 6× into a double-stacked enemy. Near-theoretical maximum damage.
T4 · Cleanup
Remaining SSRs clean up survivors.
Weakness: Wall First opponents may arrive fresh at T3. Compensated by the raw damage multiplier.
#6
Guard First
Neutralise their carry rotation before your big team arrives.
Guards Hitters
▸
T1 · Guard Wall
Both guards lead. Opponent's AoE and Carry cooldowns waste on protected targets.
T2 · Debuffs + SSRs
Press the advantage — opponent lost momentum in T1.
T3 · Main Hitters
Carries arrive to a disrupted opponent who already spent their big cooldowns.
T4 · Second Offense
Strong counter to AoE-heavy T1 opponents.
#7
Endurance Wall
Durable openers soak the enemy's debuffs and alpha strike, then your carries land clean.
Endurance Carries
▸
The problem with thin openers is they die to the first hit and trigger the opponent's death passives. Endurance Wall opens with medium-strength (100–300K) SSRs that have Endurance — they survive a lethal hit at 1 HP, so they last through the opponent's opening wave. An Attack All opener is ideal: it spreads your damage AND soaks debuffs across the whole enemy board. Once the opponent has burned their debuffs and big cooldowns on your wall, your real carries drop into T2 and hit clean.
T1 · Endurance Wall
Durable 100–300K SSRs (Attack All first). Survive the opening, soak debuffs.
T2 · Carries + Guard
Top carries behind a guard, landing clean after the enemy spent their opening.
T3 · Debuffs + Entry Guard
Spaced debuffs and an entry guard cut incoming damage in the later waves.
T4 · Durable Cleanup
More durable bodies that survive to the end instead of feeding death passives.
Best vs: opponents who front-load debuffs or rely on death-passive chains. Key cards: any SSR with Endurance, Life Flip, or Survivor's Burst — Attack All is a bonus.
Credit: Aya
#8
Dream Hunt
Sleep the enemy's big cards, then hit the sleeping target for 6x.
Sleep Dream Hunt
▸
Dream Hunt deals 6x damage to a Sleeping enemy — one of the highest single-target hits in the game. The combo: a Sleep Entry SSR enters from reinforcements and puts a random enemy to sleep, then a Dream Hunt carry deletes that sleeping target. The catch is that sleep fodder must be leveled — weak sleep cards die before they can sleep the opponent's big cards, leaving you to sleep their filler instead. So this strategy intentionally sorts sleep fodder strongest-first, the opposite of the debuff strategies.
T1 · Leveled Sleep Fodder
Strongest sleep cards first — they must last long enough to sleep enemy carries.
T2 · Sleep Entry + Dream Hunt
Sleep Entry SSR (reinforcement) sleeps a big enemy; Dream Hunt carry hits 6x.
T3 · More Sleep Pairing
Second Sleep Entry + Dream Hunt pairing behind a guard for the next wave.
T4 · Cleanup
Leftover sleep fodder and heavy hitters mop up.
Best vs: opponents with one dominant carry you need to neutralise. Key cards: Sleep Entry SSRs (Quiet Afternoon Jiro, Kingly Adornment Jin, Mono Yearning Rui) + any Dream Hunt carry. Level your sleep cards.
Credit: Aya

Singular gems (PATK, SATK, ATK, HP, PDEF, SDEF) give more power per level. Combined gems (STR, INT, VIT, TEN) boost 2 stats but at lower efficiency per level — use on secondary cards to free singulars for your main carry.
💎 Gem Progression Tips
📌 Fragment trade — do it only once. Your first gem fragment trade rewards 20 diamonds and counts toward life missions. Trade for a Lv 7–9 gem and stop — repeated trades give no bonus.
📌 Early game target: Lv 5 gems. A Lv 5 gem provides enough of a power boost to clear early Galaxy Express cases and get you through initial progression.
📌 Late game target: Lv 9+ gems. Once your warding cards hold Lv 3+ gems, start saving for Lv 9. Owning a Lv 9 gem and a Lv 11 gem each unlock milestone rewards — plan your upgrades around hitting those thresholds.
📌 Lv 9 is the highest you can buy — further upgrades are done through the gem upgrade system, not purchases.
